VAW-FREE COMMUNITY ADVOCACY

Where Can I Seek Help?

You are not alone. If you or someone you know is experiencing abuse, harassment, or violence, support and help are available.

Important: If someone is in immediate danger, move to a safer place if possible and contact emergency services or a trusted person. Avoid confronting an abusive person if it could increase danger.
National Emergency Hotline 911 Tap to call when available on your device

Find the Kind of Help You Need

🏘️ Barangay & Local Social Services

Barangay VAW Desk — Visit the VAW Desk at your barangay hall for assistance, referrals, and information about protection options.

Local Social Welfare Office — Your city or municipal social welfare office can help connect you with social workers and support services.

Ask a trusted person to accompany you if you feel safer doing so.

🏥 Medical Support

Women and Children Protection Unit (WCPU) — Hospital-based teams may provide medical assessment, care, and referrals for women and children who experienced abuse.

Ask the nearest hospital if it has a Women and Children Protection Unit or a social worker who can help with referrals.

If You're Supporting Someone

  • Listen and take their concern seriously.
  • Do not blame, shame, or pressure them.
  • Ask what support would feel helpful and respect their choices.
  • Help them connect with a trusted adult or qualified support service.
  • Keep their personal information private, unless safety requires help from a responsible professional.
